WHY NOW
Confirmed on main @ 1e91f897. Cuttingboard has a canonical decision PRODUCER (the daily
pipeline that materializes logs/latest_contract.json plus the post-gate TradeDecision
set at runtime/__init__.py:1012-1055, and the daily run summary at :1705-1768) but NO
canonical carrier of EFFECTIVE PERMISSION over time. Every permission-shaped defect in
the Astra/Sol forensic packet is the same shape: a downstream consumer independently
reconstructs permission-like semantics from a proxy (notify_mode, tradable, posture,
filename, or publish arrival order) instead of reading one resolved state. Reproduced on
this exact SHA: F7 hourly NO_TRADE placeholder rendered as STAY FLAT over a daily TRADE;
F3 contract viewer derives TRADE_READY from status+tradable ignoring outcome and lock;
F1 a delayed older TRADE overwrites a newer HALT on the publish branch; F2 a benign
hourly render loses a prior HALT (no restriction continuity); F5 same-second
generation-id collision and split latest bundle; F8 A+/IF NOW action language survives a
NO_TRADE/HALT board. The fail-closed operator lock currently masks the positive-
permission variants; an AVAILABLE unlock would expose them. The minimum coherent
correction is one accepted-authority carrier that all surfaces consume and that
publication refuses to regress.

CORE INVARIANT (the rule this PRD encodes)
Effective permission is ORIGINATED or RESTORED only by an admitted, valid, fully
evaluated daily decision, projected as a single resolved carrier that names its
decision, session, operator provenance, applicable restrictions, and a monotonic
authority version. An observation may faithfully CARRY a still-valid daily grant (it
projects the same admitted decision under unchanged validity and retained restrictions)
but may never originate or restore one. Within a session, restrictions are MONOTONIC:
observations, renderers, schedulers, retries, and publication ordering may raise a
restriction or leave it unchanged, but may never lower one, originate a grant, or
restore an expired or revoked grant. The ONLY event that lowers restriction or
re-originates permission is a new admitted full daily decision (a full redecision), which
publishes a strictly higher authority version carrying an explicit recovery basis and
which must itself satisfy every applicable restriction (a still-CANNOT_MONITOR operator
setting is not cleared by a redecision). Every permission-asserting surface consumes this
one carrier; publication accepts a bundle only if its authority version is not behind the
accepted one, it does not lower restriction WITHIN the same decision, and (for an equal
authority version) it carries the same decision_uid as the accepted carrier. A lower
restriction is accepted ONLY across a strictly higher decision_seq carrying a validated
recovery_basis (an authorized R7 recovery); a different decision at an equal version is
refused or reconciled, never accepted as an idempotent replay.

GOAL
Introduce one dedicated persisted accepted-authority carrier plus an effective_permission
projection, composed once at the daily decision boundary and carried forward (never
re-originated) by hourly observations, so that the board headline, the action directives,
the contract viewer, Telegram, and the publisher all obey a single monotonic
effective-permission state, and no observation, render, retry, scheduler, stale artifact,
or publish ordering can grant, resurrect, or regress permission.

CARRIER REUSE (why a new persisted file, reversing the v1 preference)
The v1 design reused logs/latest_run.json as the prior-accepted read source. Verified on
this SHA that reuse cannot satisfy the invariant: cuttingboard.yml "Restore publish state"
restores audit/evaluation/last_notification_state/market_map/latest_hourly_contract/
regime_history/run_*.json but NOT latest_run.json or latest_contract.json (the daily
decision carriers are never restored); and hourly_alert.yml restores latest_run.json
read-only and reverts it (:222), writing only latest_hourly_run.json (:2537-2538). So no
existing restored carrier persists cross-run effective permission. A single dedicated
accepted-authority file, added to both restore lists, is the smallest carrier that both
lanes read and write; overloading latest_run/latest_contract (changing their restore and
revert semantics) is more invasive. This satisfies the charge's escape clause ("prefer
reuse UNLESS reuse cannot satisfy the invariant").

EFFECTIVE_PERMISSION CARRIER (schema)
Stored on the dedicated accepted-authority file AND projected onto the daily/hourly run
summary and the contract root:
- state:            PERMITTED | NO_TRADE | OBSERVE_ONLY | HALT | UNAVAILABLE
- decision_uid:     the unique originating daily run_uid (identifies the immutable
                    admitted decision; preserved across observations)
- session_date:     ET calendar date (exchange/session identity, per PRD-338)
- origin_event:     daily_decision | observation
- operator_provenance: ci_repo_var | local_env | absent (the channel that resolved the
                    frozen operator availability; provenance is descriptive and does NOT
                    by itself establish production admission)
- restriction_rank: 0 PERMITTED < 1 NO_TRADE < 2 OBSERVE_ONLY(lock) < 3 HALT.
                    UNAVAILABLE is not a rank; it is the fail-closed resolution when
                    authority is absent/malformed/prior-session/expired and suppresses all
                    action language. Integrity/MIXED_ARTIFACTS is retained as a restriction
                    at HALT rank.
- authority_version: [session_date, decision_seq, restriction_rank]. decision_seq is the
                    count of admitted daily decisions this session, read from the restored
                    accepted-authority carrier and incremented ONLY by a new daily
                    decision. Within a decision, restriction_rank only rises. This is the
                    sole ordering key; generated_at is never the ordering key.
- run_uid:          unique per invocation (breaks the mode+second generation collision).
- as_of:            decision authority time (of the originating daily decision).
- valid_until:      validity boundary (session close by default; any expiry is Gate-A Q2),
                    preserved unchanged through observations.
- recovery_basis:   null unless origin_event=daily_decision lowered restriction; then a
                    validated reference to the redecision and the accepted authority/
                    restrictions it supersedes.

REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Origination vs carrying
effective_permission.state == PERMITTED is ORIGINATED or RESTORED only by a daily run
(origin_event == daily_decision) whose finalized decision had a surviving actionable
candidate (:1051-1055). An observation (origin_event == observation) may CARRY a
PERMITTED state only by projecting the same decision_uid under unchanged valid_until and
without lowering restriction_rank; it may never originate or restore one.
FAIL: an observation sets PERMITTED with a new/absent decision_uid, changes valid_until,
or a daily run with no actionable candidate carries PERMITTED.

R2 — Single-carrier headline; fail-closed when absent
The rendered decision-state (data-raw-state / data-raw-title) is derived from
effective_permission.state whenever the field is present. When the field is absent or
malformed, permission-asserting surfaces resolve UNAVAILABLE (STATE UNAVAILABLE), NOT a
byte-identical legacy outcome mapping that could show a grant; legacy decision content
may be shown only as explicitly historical, non-current context.
FAIL: with a present state X, data-raw-state disagrees with the canonical mapping of X;
OR an absent/malformed carrier renders TRADE PERMITTED / a current directional grant.

R3 — Hourly observation cannot re-author the verdict
Given a daily effective_permission with state == PERMITTED and a same-session hourly
observation, the hourly render reflects the carried state per the Gate-A session-validity
policy (Q1) and NEVER STAY FLAT derived from the hourly placeholder outcome. (If Helm
selects observation-time downgrade to OBSERVE_ONLY, this requirement and its expected
mapping are amended to that policy at Gate A.)
FAIL: the hourly render emits STAY FLAT / NO TRADE sourced from the hourly placeholder
while the carried daily state is PERMITTED (today's reproduced F7).

R4 — Restriction monotonicity within a session
Within one session_date, the accepted restriction_rank is non-decreasing across
observation runs. A benign hourly carries the prior rank unchanged; an hourly with an
operator lock or HALT/kill-switch raises it. carry_forward computes
max(prior_accepted_rank, this_run_operator_lock_rank, this_run_halt_rank).
FAIL: an accepted same-session observation lowers restriction_rank (e.g. a prior HALT or
lock becomes NO_TRADE/PERMITTED after a benign hourly), or a benign hourly discards a
prior HALT (reproduced F2/D).

R5 — Same-run HALT precedence preserved
Given effective_permission.state == HALT for the current run, the render emits HALT /
SYSTEM HALT regardless of any carried decision_uid.
FAIL: any HALT-state artifact renders TRADE PERMITTED or STAY FLAT.

R6 — Operator lock precedence preserved and composed
A run whose frozen operator availability is CANNOT_MONITOR contributes
restriction_rank >= OBSERVE_ONLY into composition/carry_forward; state resolves
OBSERVE_ONLY (unless a higher HALT applies) and IF NOW / PLAY / A+ actionable directives
are suppressed. Availability alone never upgrades an old blocked decision; a redecision
does not clear a still-CANNOT_MONITOR setting.
FAIL: a locked run renders TRADE PERMITTED, emits IF NOW / PLAY / A+ ACTIONABLE, or a
redecision produces PERMITTED while the operator setting is CANNOT_MONITOR.

R7 — Explicit recovery only
restriction_rank is lowered (or PERMITTED restored after a restriction) ONLY on a daily
run with origin_event == daily_decision, a strictly higher authority_version (incremented
decision_seq), and a non-null validated recovery_basis referencing the superseded
authority. No observation, render, retry, scheduler invocation, or stale artifact lowers
it; a greater version plus a non-null string alone is insufficient without a valid
recovery_basis and satisfied restrictions.
FAIL: any non-daily-decision event, or a daily decision lacking an incremented
decision_seq or a valid recovery_basis, lowers restriction_rank or restores PERMITTED.

R8 — Publication non-regression (publish-branch bytes)
The guard compares the incoming authority_version against the origin/publish carrier
lexicographically (session_date, then decision_seq, then restriction_rank) and admits the
overlay of permission-STATE files ONLY if ALL hold:
(a) not behind: incoming session_date/decision_seq is not older than the accepted one;
(b) no intra-decision downgrade: at an EQUAL decision_seq, restriction_rank is not lowered;
(c) recovery-gated downgrade: a lower restriction_rank is admitted ONLY when incoming
    decision_seq is strictly higher AND carries a validated recovery_basis (an authorized
    R7 recovery), so a legitimate HALT->PERMITTED redecision is NOT rejected;
(d) identity-qualified equality: an EQUAL authority_version is accepted as idempotent
    replay ONLY when incoming decision_uid == the accepted carrier's decision_uid
    (faithful replay / observation of the same decision; observation run_uid may differ).
    A DIFFERENT decision_uid at an equal authority_version is REFUSED/reconciled under R10,
    never accepted as replay.
A future/stale as_of/valid_until or a split bundle (R9) is refused. The guard runs on the
bootstrap path and inside each retry attempt against the freshly-fetched tip. It classifies
the ACTUAL prospective staged/overlaid publish delta -- including the static ui/* sync from
origin/main (ci_push_artifacts.sh:118,130), not merely git diff PRE_SHA..POST_SHA -- and
enforces (a)-(d) whenever any permission-STATE file (ui/contract.json, ui/dashboard.html,
ui/index.html, latest_run/contract/payload, the accepted-authority carrier) is in that
delta; refreshing static viewer CODE (ui/app.js, css) to current main is not a state
regression but must not carry a state file past classification. Audit/macro-only/non-state
publishes are unaffected. This covers publish-branch bytes; served-Pages deployment
ordering is DEFERRED.
FAIL: a behind / intra-decision-lower-restriction / non-recovery-downgrade / equal-version-
different-decision / older-session / future / split permission-STATE bundle overwrites the
accepted carrier; OR a valid higher-decision_seq recovery is rejected; OR the guard is
bypassed on the bootstrap path or by the static-UI sync; OR a legitimate macro-only/
audit-only publish is refused.

R9 — Accepted-bundle identity
The daily/hourly run summary, contract, payload, and accepted-authority file written by
one invocation share the same run_uid and authority_version; the guard refuses a split
(mismatched run_uid / authority_version across the permission-STATE set). For an equal
authority_version, the guard additionally requires the incoming decision_uid to match the
accepted carrier's decision_uid (R8(d)); intra-bundle agreement alone is insufficient.
FAIL: latest_run.json and latest_contract.json carrying different run_uid /
authority_version are published together (reproduced F5 split-bundle); OR an equal-version
bundle with a decision_uid different from the accepted carrier is published as a replay.

R10 — Identity sufficiency and version allocation
Two invocations in the same wall-clock second produce distinct run_uid. authority_version
is allocated from the restored accepted-authority carrier (decision_seq from the prior
accepted decision; a new daily decision increments it) and validated at publish by
compare-and-swap against the freshly-fetched tip, so an older computation cannot become
"newer" by receiving a sequence at completion/retry, and two concurrent decisions reading
the same prior version are serialized at publish: because equal-version acceptance is
identity-qualified (R8(d)/R9), the second distinct decision (same allocated
[session_date, decision_seq, restriction_rank] but a different decision_uid) is refused or
reconciled against the freshly-fetched tip, not accepted as a replay and not silently
rebased.
FAIL: two same-second invocations share a run_uid; ordering uses generated_at; a
completion-time-allocated older decision publishes over a newer accepted one; OR two
distinct daily decisions that allocated the same authority_version from the same prior
carrier both publish (the second overwrites the first) (reproduced F5/J).

R11 — Viewer obeys the carrier (every block)
Every permission-like ui/app.js block resolves from effective_permission.state: the signal
bar (no TRADE_READY from status+tradable), renderNoTrade/renderWatchlist, the primary and
secondary trade blocks (hidden unless PERMITTED, regardless of candidate presence), and
the payload outcome badge. Absent/malformed field -> non-actionable UNAVAILABLE.
FAIL: any viewer block shows TRADE_READY, a "Primary Trade"/direction/entry/strategy/R:R,
or a TRADE outcome badge while effective_permission.state is not PERMITTED, or for an
absent/malformed carrier (reproduced F3).

R12 — Fail-closed authority (absent/malformed/prior-session/expired)
When effective_permission is absent, malformed, references a prior session_date, or is
past valid_until, every consumer (renderer, viewer, Telegram) resolves UNAVAILABLE and
suppresses action language, rather than fabricating NO_TRADE or retaining a positive
grant. Consumer-side expiry applies even when no new render succeeds (a served/open page
past valid_until shows UNAVAILABLE); publish-time rejection alone does not expire served
bytes. No exception escapes the renderer or the guard. BOOTSTRAP: on the first session
with no accepted-authority carrier on either main or publish (e.g. an hourly-first
invocation before any daily decision), consumers render UNAVAILABLE and the producer does
NOT synthesize an observation carrier or an allocatable decision_seq; the carrier is first
written only by the first admitted daily decision, so "write the carrier back" never
manufactures observation authority.
FAIL: a missing/malformed/prior-session/expired carrier yields PERMITTED, a fabricated
NO_TRADE presented as a current decision, a served page still asserting a grant past
valid_until, or an unhandled exception.

R13 — Action language consistent with the effective verdict
IF NOW, PLAY, and A+ ACTIONABLE (renderer) and READY / direction / candidate action lines
/ "Tradable: Yes" (Telegram) are emitted only when effective_permission.state == PERMITTED.
