GOAL
Deliver the daily Market Control Card as a read-only decision-support surface:
seven fields — LOCATION, STATE, PERMISSION, EVENT, TRANSITION, INVALIDATION,
CANDIDATE-IMPLICATION — each a truthful value or a typed UNAVAILABLE carrying
a reason token from a closed per-field vocabulary, composed once per eligible
daily run by a builder that is the sole producer of every card value, rendered
by a renderer that projects and never derives, defaults, or invents.

PRODUCT OUTCOME
On every eligible daily run (`MODE_LIVE` / `MODE_FIXTURE`, halted eligible
runs included; never `MODE_SUNDAY`), the daily dashboard shows one compact
card answering VISION's questions at a glance: where are we (LOCATION), what
is the market's state (STATE), what am I permitted to do (PERMISSION), what
is on the calendar (EVENT), what changed intraday (TRANSITION), is anything
invalidated (INVALIDATION), and what does this imply for today's candidates
(CANDIDATE-IMPLICATION). No prediction; nothing invented; unavailability is
explicit and typed.

COMPOSER SEMANTICS (binding)
- The builder is the SOLE producer of every final card value; the renderer
  projects and never derives, defaults, or invents.
- The composer contract is defined over ELIGIBLE runs only: daily
  `MODE_LIVE`/`MODE_FIXTURE` executions (halted eligible runs included). On
  `MODE_SUNDAY` the composer is not invoked and the card does not exist — no
  Sunday token; the payload section is omitted and the renderer block is
  absent (F1; M23).
- All card-side time logic is keyed on `run_at_utc` — never wall-clock
  `now()` — so the card is deterministic for a given run.
- Every normalization above is total and deterministic; empty-vs-unavailable
