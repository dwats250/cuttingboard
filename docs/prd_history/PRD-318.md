R2 — Adjacent Tape, never synthesized judgment
Tape shows only existing macro bias/votes, a display-only exact count over existing Trend Structure alignment rows, qualified GEX context when valid, participation availability, and existing driver values.
FAIL: Tape changes any source value or emits ALIGNED, DIVERGING, CONFLUENT, an agreement color/score, or a synthesized market verdict.

R3 — Today preserves independent time truth
Today groups existing red-folder, session, and Sunday facts with compact honest empty/unavailable states while each underlying provenance clock remains independently available.
FAIL: Today invents an event/session rule, hides a critical unavailable/stale state, or collapses independent clocks into one asserted as-of time.

R4 — Watching preserves independent semantics
Watching keeps Opportunity -> Candidate -> alert continuity; candidate grades are explicitly screening observations, map-empty and qualification-empty use distinct wording, and all candidate content remains accessible under every decision state.
FAIL: the two empty concepts share wording, a grade resembles permission, any candidate computation changes, or non-permitted presentation removes candidate content from the DOM.

Details retains full Trend, Movement, Macro, GEX, SPY session, Changes, Scoreboard, diagnostics/provenance; SPY/MCC permission and ORB duplicate projections may render once while their carriers remain unchanged.
FAIL: required evidence disappears from the DOM, a non-identical fact is removed, or payload/carrier output changes.

R6 — Closed operator language on touched surfaces
Known SPY/MCC/candidate empty/failure tokens and touched raw timestamps render through tested operator wording while exact raw values remain available as data attributes where needed.
FAIL: the tested touched surfaces visibly leak a covered raw enum/ISO timestamp or translate two different candidate concepts to the same phrase.

R7 — Responsive and semantic parity
At 390x844 the authoritative decision is first, context and Watching move materially upward, critical information remains reachable, and 360/390/430/1280 viewports have zero horizontal overflow without changing text/source facts across widths.
FAIL: browser checks show overflow, inaccessible critical content, desktop degradation, or viewport-dependent fact/text mutation.
