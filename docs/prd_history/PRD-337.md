WHY NOW
NS-4C Leadership needs a stable, owner-authored measurement universe and an
observation carrier before any relative-performance computation can be built.
This PRD is the PRECURSOR that lands that carrier and nothing more: it evolves
the legacy 12-symbol mixed-purpose registry into a bounded 22-symbol
MARKET-STRUCTURE MEASUREMENT universe, wires a best-effort observation-only fetch
seam, and carries the observations to the MARKET MOVEMENT card. It computes NO
leadership, NO breadth, and NO relative performance. It adds NO decision
authority. NS-4C (Leadership) and NS-4D (Breadth) remain future work and are NOT
authorized by this slice.

Authored from Helm's PRD-337 precursor charge (2026-09-06). Design basis:
audits/prd-337-market-structure-universe-stage0-2026-09/FABLE_REVIEW.md (the
commissioned Fable 5.1 design review, ACCEPT WITH CHANGES), which supersedes the
original 12-row benchmark ratification proposal.

ACCEPTED PRODUCT SCOPE
NS-4A v2 / Market Structure Universe -> prepares the carrier for NS-4C Leadership
-> NS-4D Breadth remains later. This slice is the first arrow only.

REQUIRED_SYMBOLS, HALT_SYMBOLS, NON_TRADABLE_SYMBOLS, and
TREND_STRUCTURE_SYMBOLS. They never enter validate_quotes, valid_quotes, derived,
structure, regime, candidates, qualification, notification counts, ranking,
permission, or execution. Proven by a real-stage decision-invariance paired run.
