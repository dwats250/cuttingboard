every decision state whenever a valid primary exists, while every decision,
permission, secondary-chart, ladder, styling, A1, and staleness rule renders
exactly as it does today.

REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Primary chart undisclosed in every decision state
When the card whose symbol equals the renderer's `select_primary_card_symbol`
result produces a non-empty chart SVG (daily, or admitted intraday per
PRD-324), `_render_candidate_card` emits `<div class="setup-chart">` and its
`chart-caption` as direct children of that card, with no `<details` token
between the card's opening `id="card-<SYM>"` tag and that chart, under every
decision state: TRADE PERMITTED, STAY FLAT, OBSERVE ONLY (operator lock),
HALT, and STATE UNAVAILABLE. The chart keeps its current position in the card
(after the IN/OUT couplet and the DETAIL disclosure, before the LEVEL MAP
disclosure), which is where it already renders on a permitted card.
FAIL: for any non-permitted render with a chartable primary, the substring
between `id="card-<primary>"` and the first `class="setup-chart"` in that card
contains `<details`; OR `_charts_by_details_depth` on a two-high-grade-symbol
non-permitted render is not exactly `[0, 2]` (primary at 0, secondary behind
`level-detail` then `chart-detail`).

R2 — Ladder, DETAIL, tier, and secondary behaviour unchanged
On non-permitted renders `<details class="level-detail"><summary>LEVEL MAP ▶</summary>`
still opens immediately after the primary chart block and
wraps the compact ladder; on permitted renders no `level-detail` wrapper exists
(today's behaviour). Every non-primary card is byte-identical to today in every
state: its chart stays inside `chart-detail` (inside `level-detail` when not
permitted); no secondary chart opens. Tier-group disclosures, the DETAIL
disclosure, IF NOW, PLAY, and the IN/OUT couplet are untouched.
FAIL: a non-permitted render lacks `<details class="level-detail">` in the
primary card or renders `class="lvl-ladder` outside it; OR a permitted render
contains `level-detail`; OR any non-primary card's HTML differs from the
pre-D1 render for the same inputs; OR `_charts_by_details_depth` on a
three-symbol permitted render is not `[0, 1, 1]`.

R3 — Existing neutral presentation on every non-permitted render (chart and ladder)
`_render_candidate_card` derives one local boolean, `neutral =
operator_locked or not decision_permitted`, and passes it as the
`operator_locked` argument to both `render_setup_chart_svg` calls and to
`_render_level_ladder`. Effect: a non-permitted, unlocked render that carries a
contract entry/stop shows the PRD-304/PRD-321 lock presentation in the chart
(LEVEL / INVALIDATION words, `#6b7280`, no `#e0a552` / `#e05252`) and in the
ladder (`lvl-neutral`, `lvl-lockrisk`; no `lvl-entry`, `lvl-stop`,
`lvl-inrisk`). Locked renders are unchanged (already neutral). Permitted
renders are unchanged (action palette). Everything else keyed on
`operator_locked` (IF NOW, PLAY, couplet labels, `.value-actionable`) stays
keyed on `operator_locked` alone.
FAIL: a `NO_TRADE` unlocked render with `contract_entry_map` and
`contract_stop_map` set shows `>ENTRY ` or `>STOP ` or `#e0a552` or `#e05252`
inside the primary card's SVG, or `lvl-entry` / `lvl-stop` / `lvl-inrisk` in
its ladder; OR the permitted render loses `>ENTRY ` / `>STOP ` / `#e0a552` /
`#e05252` / `lvl-inrisk`; OR the lock test
`test_prd321_locked_render_neutralizes_the_chart_and_the_ladder` is edited.
