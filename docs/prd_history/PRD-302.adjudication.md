# PRD-302 — CONTRACT classification adjudication

Mandatory adjudication artifact for a CONTRACT-class PRD
(`docs/PRD_PROCESS.md` § Adjudication Trigger: "CONTRACT-class PRDs: Any
reviewer disagreement REQUIRES an adjudication artifact ... regardless of
whether the disagreement is later resolved"). Recorded even though the
disagreement is now resolved by owner ruling.

## The disagreement

- **Packet author (HELM, Opus 4.8)** classified the work `CLASS: INFRA` on
  dominant-purpose grounds: the deliverable primarily installs CI/workflow
  plumbing, and the coordination JSON schemas are internal to the control plane
  and do not redefine Cuttingboard's runtime/payload contract
  (`cuttingboard/output.py`, `ui/contract.json`, the `TradeDecision` shape).
- **Independent reviewer (Sol, `gpt-5.6-sol`, rebuild INITIAL review, Finding 5,
  pinned `ab958645`)** disagreed: the canonical closed CLASS set assigns
  "payload schema, artifact contracts, cross-module shape definitions" to
  CONTRACT, and there is **no** general "dominant-purpose" rule in canon
  (PRD-230 is precedent commentary, not a class-definition override). This
  payload deliberately introduces a JSON schema
  (`.github/campaign/charge.schema.json`) and a job-boundary artifact contract
  (the normalized-event and charge shapes) -- literal CONTRACT purposes. The
  reviewer required either reclassification to CONTRACT or an authoritative
  adjudication expressly permitting INFRA, and noted that CONTRACT mandates an
  adjudication artifact on any reviewer disagreement.

## Owner adjudication (2026-08-13): CLASS = CONTRACT

Dustin ruled **CLASS: CONTRACT** (owner ruling 2 of the Stage-0/PRD charge):
choose the conservative canonical class rather than inventing a mixed/
dominant-purpose exception. `LANE: HIGH-RISK` and MATERIAL remain binding. INFRA
is retained only as a change-surface descriptor (the workflow/plumbing surface),
not as the governing class.

### Why

- The canonical CONTRACT definition is broader than the trading payload and
  squarely covers an internal JSON schema and a cross-module/job-boundary
  artifact contract. The dominant-purpose argument has no canonical basis.
- Choosing the conservative class avoids a bespoke exception and applies the
  strictest applicable ceremony to a security-sensitive, schema-defining change.

### Consequences (bound in PRD-302)

- **Full CONTRACT ceremony** (PRD-302 R16, R17, R20): full test suite;
  field-by-field schema-diff review over every field/type/enum/pattern/length/
  required-key/`additionalProperties` rule; complete producer/consumer audit
  naming the model, handwritten validator, renderer, one-day artifact, public
  job log, OpenAI/Responses provider + retention boundary, runner command-file
  processor, and disclosed Slice-B dependencies.
- **Adjudication artifact mandatory on any reviewer disagreement** -- this
  document, plus any future disagreement during PRD or implementation review.
- CONTRACT's default tier T0 makes the `CHANGE SURFACE` section mandatory
  (present in PRD-302).

### No weakening of the INFRA workflow safeguards

Reclassifying to CONTRACT ADDS ceremony; it removes nothing. Every INFRA-flavored
safeguard the packet established is retained as a binding PRD-302 requirement:
`workflow_dispatch`-only trigger, owner-only actor + reviewed-main binding,
least-privilege `contents: read` (no `id-token`/write), secret-once + Codex
Action as the literal final step, `persist-credentials: false`,
`unprivileged-user` credential isolation with the blocking post-merge probe,
inert model-output transport, structural TRIPWIRE tests, and requested-only
model identity. The change-surface remains a new CI workflow plus its checked-in
tool, prompt, and schema.

## Disposition

Disagreement resolved: **CLASS CONTRACT**, full ceremony bound, INFRA safeguards
preserved as change-surface. This artifact satisfies the CONTRACT adjudication
requirement for the classification disagreement. It authorizes nothing
downstream; Gate A remains Dustin's.
