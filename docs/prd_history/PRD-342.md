PRD-342 — Freshness and provenance wording: neutral decision-age banner + timeframe-first chart captions

Status: IN PROGRESS

LANE
STANDARD

CLASS
CONSUMER

WHY NOW
The top staleness banner labels a still-valid carried session decision `BOARD Nh
OLD` on elapsed time alone (no freshness contract), which reads as malfunction.
Chart captions bury timeframe/provenance and can make a prior completed-daily
candle look stale. Reword display-only to distinguish decision-age from genuine
source-health staleness and make intraday-vs-daily provenance obvious, preserving
every existing freshness/source-health contract. Owner charge 2026-09-14 (Gate A
direction frozen); the deferred half of PRD-341.

MAX EXPECTED DELTA
- Production files: <= 2 (`cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py`,
  `cuttingboard/delivery/intraday_bars.py`).
- Production LOC: <= ~70 net (display strings, one banner-JS branch, one caption
  helper reword each, one `_spy_clock_line` decouple).
- No new module, dependency, artifact, schema field, threshold-that-gates-a-
  decision, or authority/permission read.

GOAL
Make the dashboard's freshness and provenance legible at a glance — a healthy
carried decision reads as a neutral `DECISION · Nh`, genuine staleness keeps its
existing warnings, and each chart states its timeframe and completed-through — with
zero change to any decision, permission, setup, ingestion, or scheduling behavior.

SCOPE
- Reword the top staleness banner's aged-but-valid branch from the warning `BOARD
  Nh OLD` to a neutral `DECISION · Nh`; keep the UNAVAILABLE and MARKET CLOSED
  branches; keep the banner's inform-never-instruct contract.
- Reword the daily chart caption to timeframe-first `1D · through <Weekday Mon D>`,
  preserving machine-readable source/interval provenance.
- Reword the intraday chart caption to timeframe-first `5m · through <h:MM AM/PM>
  ET`, preserving the PRD-324 R10 ET session_date and completed-through END.
- Decouple `_spy_clock_line` from re-parsing the daily caption string (pass the ISO
  `as_of` directly), so the caption can be reworded without breaking the SPY clock
  line, whose visible output is unchanged.

CURRENT BEHAVIOR (traced; the exact thresholds and state transitions this PRD reasons from)

Banner (`_STALENESS_BANNER_JS`, client-side, dashboard_renderer.py). Server ships
`#staleness-banner` empty+hidden with `data-session-inactive` and
`data-board-stale-after-s` (= `BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` = 90*60 = 5400). The JS,
at view time, reads `#cb-updated data-updated-utc` (= pipeline run/publish time)
and computes `ageSec = (now - updated)/1000` against the VIEWER clock, then:
  1. no/NaN timestamp        -> `LAST UPDATE: UNAVAILABLE` (grey #888)
  2. data-session-inactive   -> `MARKET CLOSED · LAST UPDATE {age} AGO` (grey #888)
  3. ageSec > 5400 (90 min)  -> `BOARD {age} OLD` (WARNING colour) ← the problem
  4. else                    -> hidden
It reads NO valid_until, NO session-expiry, NO decision-validity contract. It is
"time since the board was last regenerated," not "the decision is no longer valid."
`data-session-inactive` is Sunday-premarket-only (INACTIVE_SESSION_TYPES =
{"SUNDAY_PREMARKET"}), not a general market-open/closed clock.

Genuine freshness/source-health contracts (ALL independent of the banner; ALL
preserved by this PRD):
  - Publish gate `StalePublishError` (LIVE 180 min / INACTIVE 72h) — SERVER-ONLY,
    refuses to publish a stale board (so any on-screen board was fresh at publish).
  - `_compute_timestamp_freshness` (300s) -> `_system_state_source_health`
    (OK/STALE/INVALID/MIXED) — client-visible via the system-state block.
  - market_map mtime staleness (300s) -> mm_health.
  - price-bars 5-calendar-day guard -> chart absent + caption reflects `as_of`.
  - intraday 90-min / 5-min future-skew guard -> daily chart substitutes.
  - `#spy-observation` session-validity (`data-raw-state` STALE / session_mismatch)
    -> "Session read stale · intended <when>".

Daily caption (`_price_bars_caption`) -> `bars through {as_of} · {provider}
{interval}` (e.g. `bars through 2026-09-11 · yfinance 1d`). `_spy_clock_line`
RE-PARSES this string: `as_of = caption.split("bars through ", 1)[1][:10]` to emit
`Levels updated <clock> · daily bars through <Mon D>` — a brittle prose coupling.

Intraday caption (`intraday_bars._caption`) -> `{session_date.isoformat()} intraday
5m - completed through {completed_end} ET`. PRD-324 R10: the caption MUST state the
ET session_date and the completed-through END, and MUST NOT caption the raw source
`through`.

DECISION POINTS (owner-held; recommendations first)

D1 — Does the BANNER itself escalate for a genuinely-expired decision?
  RECOMMENDATION: NO (this PRD). Elapsed time alone cannot honestly separate
  "genuinely stale" from "old because the market is closed" (there is no client-
  side market-open signal beyond Sunday-premarket). The only truthful expiry signal
  is `effective_permission.valid_until` — which is inert until Slice 2 and whose
  designated first consumer is the IN-PROGRESS PRD-340 (Authority Projection).
  Consuming it here would reopen authority architecture (the charge forbids this),
  preempt PRD-340, and re-classify this PRD MATERIAL. So the banner reports
  DECISION age NEUTRALLY, and genuine staleness stays with the existing detectors
  above (source-health STALE token, `#spy-observation` session-validity, publish
  gate) — all unchanged and visibly distinct.
  ALTERNATIVE (owner may direct): bake `valid_until` (reachable renderer-only via
  `_pipeline_run` = latest_run.json, zero pipeline edits) onto the banner so it
  self-escalates when `now > valid_until`. This is the first Slice-2 consumer of
  the authority envelope -> MATERIAL, a separate packet/PRD, NOT this STANDARD one.

D2 — Intraday caption and PRD-324 R10.
  RECOMMENDATION: keep a COMPACT visible session_date to stay R10-compliant, e.g.
  `5m · <Mon D> · through 2:00 PM ET`. ALTERNATIVE (owner ruling to narrow R10):
  drop the visible date and carry session_date machine-readably (`data-session-
  date`), matching the charge's date-less `5m · through 2:00 PM ET` example. Both
  keep the completed-through END and exclude the raw source `through`.

D3 — `_spy_clock_line` decouple.
  RECOMMENDATION: extend `_price_bars_by_symbol`'s per-symbol tuple to `(bars,
  caption, as_of)` and pass the ISO `as_of` directly into `_spy_clock_line`
  (renderer-only; the SPY clock line's visible output is byte-unchanged). This
  removes the `caption.split("bars through ")` prose parse permanently.

REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Neutral decision-age banner.
When the board is aged past `BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` in an active (non-inactive)
session and the timestamp is present, the banner reads a neutral `DECISION · Nh`
(subdued, NOT `var(--color-warning)`, NOT the word OLD, NOT STALE, no imperative
verb). The `data-board-stale-after-s` attribute still equals
`BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` (= 90*60).
FAIL: an aged same-session board renders `BOARD Nh OLD` or any warning-coloured
banner; or `data-board-stale-after-s != 5400`.

R2 — Genuine staleness and source-health escalation preserved.
Every existing freshness/source-health contract (publish gate, `_system_state_
source_health` STALE/INVALID, market_map mtime, price-bars 5-day, intraday 90-min,
`#spy-observation` STALE/session_mismatch) is byte-for-byte unchanged and still
renders its warning-visible state.
FAIL: any of those contracts is removed, weakened, or its warning no longer
renders.

R3 — Banner inform-never-instruct contract retained.
The banner JS contains no imperative verb and no bare `STALE`; it still surfaces the
UNAVAILABLE and MARKET CLOSED states.
FAIL: the banner JS contains an imperative verb or a bare `STALE`, or drops the
UNAVAILABLE/MARKET CLOSED branch.

R4 — Daily caption timeframe-first, provenance preserved.
The daily chart caption reads `1D · through <Weekday Mon D>` (e.g. `1D · through Fri
Sep 11`); the source/interval provenance remains machine-readable (data attribute
or equivalent), and a prior completed-daily candle is NOT labelled stale.
FAIL: the daily caption omits the timeframe or the completed-through date, presents
the completed-daily bar as stale, or drops machine-readable provenance.

R5 — Intraday caption timeframe-first, R10 preserved.
The intraday chart caption reads timeframe-first `5m · through <h:MM AM/PM> ET`,
states the ET session_date (visibly per D2 recommendation, else machine-readably),
carries the completed-through END, and never captions the raw source `through`.
FAIL: the intraday caption drops the session_date, drops/omits the completed-through
END, or exposes the raw source `through`.

R6 — `_spy_clock_line` decoupled, output unchanged.
`_spy_clock_line` no longer parses the caption string; its visible output
(`Levels updated <clock> · daily bars through <Mon D>`) is byte-identical to today.
FAIL: `_spy_clock_line` still calls `caption.split("bars through ")`, or its visible
output changes.

R7 — No decision/permission/setup/provenance-truth change.
No `cuttingboard` decision module is read or written differently; the change is
display-string + banner-branch + one renderer-internal tuple only. Machine-readable
provenance is unchanged or strengthened, never removed.
FAIL: any decision, permission, setup, ingestion, scheduling, or persisted-artifact
behaviour changes; or machine-readable provenance is reduced.
