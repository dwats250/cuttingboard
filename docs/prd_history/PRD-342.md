GOAL
Make the dashboard's freshness and provenance legible at a glance — a healthy
carried decision reads as a neutral `DECISION · Nh`, a genuinely EXPIRED decision
(viewer clock past `valid_until`) keeps a warning, and each chart states its
timeframe and completed-through — with zero change to any decision, permission,
setup, ingestion, or scheduling behavior.

Banner (`_STALENESS_BANNER_JS`, client-side): server ships `#staleness-banner`
empty+hidden with `data-session-inactive` (Sunday-premarket only) and
`data-board-stale-after-s` (= `BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` = 5400). The JS reads
`#cb-updated data-updated-utc` (publish time) and computes viewer-clock `ageSec`,
then: no/NaN ts -> `LAST UPDATE: UNAVAILABLE` (grey); `data-session-inactive` ->
`MARKET CLOSED · LAST UPDATE {age} AGO` (grey); `ageSec > 5400` -> `BOARD {age}
OLD` (WARNING); else hidden. It reads NO decision-validity signal.

Authority admission is ALREADY consumed: `_auth_proj =
authority_projection.admit_projection(run, current_session_date=_sess)` at :3014
(the renderer is a registered CROSS_PROCESS_EMITTER). `admit_projection` routes the
carrier's `effective_permission` through Slice-1 `admit_persisted`, which fail-
closes on absent/malformed/prior-session to the UNAVAILABLE projection. (This call
passes NO `now`, so the `now > valid_until` staleness rejection is NOT applied here

IMPORTANT: `admit_projection` returns an `ActionProjection` that does NOT carry
`valid_until` (authority_projection.py:66). The admitted `valid_until` is exposed by
`admit_ep(carrier, current_session_date=_sess)` (same `admit_persisted` admission,
returns the admitted `EffectivePermission.valid_until`; `unavailable()` sets it to
`None`). Called with `now=None`, `admit_persisted` SKIPS the `now > valid_until`
staleness rejection (effective_permission.py:229-232) — so a canonically-valid
envelope is admitted even when already expired, and its `valid_until` survives for
the CLIENT clock to classify (Sol N2); absent/malformed/prior-session still fail
closed (:224). The raw `run["effective_permission"]["valid_until"]` exists but is
UNADMITTED — the design must not read it (Sol N1). (Live admitted value verified:
`2026-09-15T08:00:00+00:00`.)

REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Honest decision-age banner (neutral when valid, warning when expired).
With a present timestamp and an active (non-inactive) session:
- viewer-clock `now > valid_until` (genuine expiry) -> `BOARD Nh OLD`, warning
  colour (retains "OLD"; this is the genuine-stale escalation).
- else, aged past `BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` -> `DECISION · Nh`, neutral colour
  (subdued; not warning; not "OLD"; not bare "STALE"; no imperative verb).
- else -> hidden.
`data-board-stale-after-s` still equals `BOARD_STALE_AFTER_SECONDS` (=5400). When
`valid_until` is absent or unparseable, the banner MUST NOT fabricate an expiry
warning (NO secondary validity clock — owner ruling 1) and MUST NOT over-claim
validity; it degrades to the plain age indicator (neutral `DECISION · Nh` if aged,
else hidden). The fail-closed authority signal for absent/malformed/invalid
authority stays on the EXISTING admission/decision-state surface
(`admit_projection` -> UNAVAILABLE), which this PRD leaves untouched.
FAIL: an aged same-session board WITHIN `valid_until` renders a warning; OR an
EXPIRED board (now > valid_until) renders no warning; OR the banner fabricates an
expiry warning when `valid_until` is absent; OR `data-board-stale-after-s != 5400`.

R2 — Every existing freshness/health contract byte-preserved (Sol F5 precision).
Publish gate, price-bars 5-day, intraday 90-min, `#spy-observation` session-
validity, and macro/trend/mm health are byte-for-byte unchanged. The design does
NOT rely on `_sys_health` being visible (it is dead; restoring it is out of scope).
FAIL: any of those contracts changes behaviour, OR the design asserts visibility
for a transition that is publish-blocking/suppression/substitution/dead rather than
a visible warning.

R3 — Banner inform-never-instruct contract retained.
The banner JS contains no imperative verb and no bare `STALE`; retains "OLD" and
"LAST UPDATE" tokens and the UNAVAILABLE/MARKET CLOSED branches.
FAIL: banner JS gains an imperative verb or bare `STALE`, or drops a required token
or branch.

R4 — Daily caption timeframe-first, provenance machine-readable (Sol F3 carrier).
The daily caption reads `1D · through <Weekday Mon D>`. `_price_bars_by_symbol`
yields `(bars, caption, as_of, provider, source_interval)` and
`_render_setup_chart_block` emits `.chart-caption` with `data-provider`,
`data-source-interval`, and `data-as-of`. Absent provenance -> the attribute is
omitted (never a fabricated value); the visible caption still shows `1D · through
<date>`. A prior completed-daily candle is NOT labelled stale.
FAIL: the daily caption omits timeframe/date; presents completed-daily as stale;
OR provider/source_interval/as_of are not machine-readable at the emission seam.

R5 — Intraday caption single grammar, R10 preserved (Sol F7).
The intraday caption reads EXACTLY `5m · <Weekday Mon D> · through <h:MM AM/PM> ET`
— stating the ET session_date and the completed-through END, never the raw source
`through`. This is the ONE grammar used in Scope, FAIL, proofs 4/8, and the manual
check. (The charge's date-less `5m · through 2:00 PM ET` is an illustrative
abbreviation, not the oracle, unless Helm narrows R10.)
FAIL: the intraday caption drops the session_date or completed-through END, or
exposes the raw source `through`, or uses any other grammar.

R6 — `_spy_clock_line` decoupled, output unchanged.
`_spy_clock_line` takes the ISO `as_of` directly (no `caption.split`); its visible
output (`Levels updated <clock> · daily bars through <Mon D>`) is byte-identical.
FAIL: `_spy_clock_line` still parses the caption, or its output changes.

R7 — No decision/permission/setup change; ADMITTED-ONLY valid_until; provenance never reduced.
No decision module is read/written differently; authority RESOLUTION and admission
SEMANTICS are untouched. `valid_until` reaches the banner ONLY via canonical
admission (`admit_ep`), never the raw envelope, so a rejected/malformed envelope
yields `valid_until=None` and the banner cannot classify from untrusted data (Sol
N1). The change DISPLAYS the admitted value and reworks display strings only.
Machine-readable provenance is unchanged or strengthened.
FAIL: the banner reads `valid_until` from a raw/unadmitted envelope; OR any
decision/permission/setup/ingestion/schedule/persisted-artifact change; OR
machine-readable provenance reduced.

DATA FLOW
pipeline_run/run `effective_permission` -> `admit_ep(current_session_date=_sess)`
(canonical + session admission, NO `now` filter) -> admitted
`EffectivePermission.valid_until` (present EVEN WHEN EXPIRED; `None` only for
absent/malformed/prior-session) -> baked `data-valid-until` on `#staleness-banner`
-> `_STALENESS_BANNER_JS` compares the VIEWER clock to the admitted value. price_bars
/ intraday snapshots -> caption helpers. No new source; no re-derivation of any
decision/authority value (the banner READS the admitted field for display); an
absent/malformed/prior-session envelope yields `valid_until=None` -> neutral banner
(never a false warning), while an expired-but-canonical envelope keeps its
`valid_until` so the client can warn.

FAIL CONDITIONS
- Aged-but-valid board warns, or expired board does not warn (R1).
- Any existing freshness/health contract behaviour changes, or a non-visible
  transition is asserted visible (R2).
- Banner JS gains imperative/bare-STALE or drops a required token/branch (R3).
- Daily caption loses timeframe/date, labels completed-daily stale, or drops
  machine-readable provider/interval/as_of (R4).
- Intraday caption departs the single R5 grammar or exposes raw through (R5).
- `_spy_clock_line` still prose-parses or changes output (R6).
- Any decision/permission/setup/artifact change or provenance reduction (R7).
