PRD-294 — Harness seat doctrine, agent seating map, and proactive-Fable supersession

Status: IN PROGRESS

LANE

WHY NOW
Harness practice for model selection, reasoning-effort selection, bounded
parallelism, and Fable consultation is fragmented and partly un-homed:
reasoning/effort has no canonical owner; the model-role split lives across three
docs; parallelism has no numeric bound; and proactive Fable conflicts with the
reactive-only owner rule. Phase-0 preflight (base ddacbf0) and a Fable
Extra-High navigator pass (verdict: REQUIRED CHANGES) converged on a two-home
architecture that codifies durable seats and invariants in their existing
binding owner (CLAUDE.md) and records mutable current occupants in one new
informative file, rather than minting a competing authority. It is a
governance-class change and lands as a visible DRAFT hold (PRD-186).

GOAL
Give the already-adopted "capability roles, not model names" principle (GOV-1)
one binding home and one informative home: durable harness seats and invariants
in CLAUDE.md; current model occupants, reasoning-effort defaults, and the
concurrency number in a new informative docs/AGENT_SEATING.md that owns no
authority; retire the provisional model-role lane through its own disposition
path; and supersede-in-part only the owner rule's orchestrator/Fable clause.

SCOPE
- Add durable harness seats (Owner, HELM/orchestrator, Builder, Navigator,
  Adversary/independent-review, mechanical/recon subagent) to CLAUDE.md,
  mapped onto the existing GOV-1 review vocabulary; add compact harness
  invariants (single HELM lane; evidence-only subordinates; no semantic vote;
  no uncontrolled shared-seam writes) and a CONDITIONAL invalidation-before-

- Create docs/AGENT_SEATING.md as an informative record of current operational

REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Durable harness seats in CLAUDE.md
CLAUDE.md gains a compact block (in § Roles) naming six durable orchestration
seats -- Owner, HELM/orchestrator, Builder, Navigator, Adversary/independent-
review, mechanical/recon subagent -- and mapping them onto the existing GOV-1
review vocabulary so no second review-seat taxonomy is created: the Adversary
seat is the already-defined independent-reviewer / commissioned second-model
capability; Navigator/HELM/Builder/mechanical are orchestration seats that carry
no review or gate authority. The block references docs/AGENT_SEATING.md as the
informative record of current occupants.

FAIL: CLAUDE.md § Roles does not name all six seats, OR it defines a review seat
that is not identically the existing GOV-1 independent-reviewer/second-model
capability, OR it omits the docs/AGENT_SEATING.md informative pointer.

R2 — Harness invariants and conditional invalidation in CLAUDE.md
CLAUDE.md gains a compact block (in § Execution posture) stating four
parallelism invariants -- one authoritative HELM/campaign lane; subordinate
agents return evidence, not semantic rulings; no semantic decisions by vote
(referencing the existing "Disagreement is Dustin's to adjudicate" rule); no
uncontrolled concurrent shared-seam writes (referencing scope locking) -- and a
CONDITIONAL invalidation-before-build practice that applies ONLY when a
non-obvious load-bearing premise could materially change FILES, an authority
boundary, semantics, blast radius, or whether the work should proceed.

FAIL: the invalidation practice is written as a universal/mandatory PRD or
template requirement, OR it introduces a new lane/tier name, OR any of the four
parallelism invariants is absent, OR the numeric concurrency cap appears in
CLAUDE.md rather than in docs/AGENT_SEATING.md.

R3 — CLAUDE.md Review gates unchanged
The CLAUDE.md § Review gates section (lines 114-226 at base ddacbf0) is
byte-identical before and after the change.

FAIL: `git diff <base>..HEAD -- CLAUDE.md` shows any added or removed line whose
position falls within the § Review gates section.

R4 — docs/AGENT_SEATING.md is an informative record, not an authority
The new file's header declares that it RECORDS current operational defaults
only, owns no authority and holds no governance facts, creates no lifecycle
gate, cannot select or waive a required gated reviewer or event, and yields to
every binding governance source on conflict (on conflict the record is wrong and
is corrected). It contains the seven seat rows, the reasoning-effort defaults,
the concurrency number (5 total, including the HELM), an explicit gated-event
carve-out (GOV-2's two Codex events, PRD-242 second-model commissioning, the CI
literals, and the lane matrix are outside its reach), and no governance history
or essay. Codex/Sol effort is stated as an economical default with selective
escalation to higher/max for consequential/sticky/experimental deep review, no
permanent ceiling, and Ultra-style/multi-agent runs as deliberate experiments.

FAIL: the file asserts it "owns" any authority or governance fact, OR states a
permanent reasoning ceiling, OR omits the gated-event carve-out, OR omits the
"5 including HELM" number, OR is added to GOVERNANCE_PAYLOAD_FILES in
tools/validate_prd_registry.py.
