REQUIREMENTS

R1 — Insertion point and gating
One step, "Refresh GEX snapshot (best-effort)", after "Aggregate regime
history" and before "Render and stage hourly artifacts", gated
`if: ${{ success() && steps.freshcheck.outputs.fresh == 'true' }}`.
FAIL: Step absent, ordered after the render step, or missing that gate.

R2 — Bounded best-effort semantics
Step body is exactly `rm -f logs/gex_snapshot.json` then
`python3 tools/gex_snapshot.py || rm -f logs/gex_snapshot.json`: producer
failure after a successful scrub degrades to ABSENCE with step exit 0; an rm
failure (inability to establish absence) is a LOCAL INFRASTRUCTURE failure and
fails the step closed (PRD-198 invariant 1; no `|| true`). Producer
exit-0-only-after-atomic-write (PRD-306 R10/R13) is relied on, not modified.
FAIL: A producer failure with working rm leaves the artifact present or the
step nonzero; OR an rm failure lets the step succeed.

R3 — Run-local artifact, pipeline untouched
logs/gex_snapshot.json enters neither the restore step's arguments nor the
commit step's `git add` set; render/readiness/commit/push steps are unchanged.
RELIED-ON INVARIANT: `logs/` stays gitignored, keeping the untracked artifact
invisible to ci_push_artifacts.sh's dirty-tree abort — guarded by R4(h).
FAIL: "gex_snapshot" appears in the restore line or the commit add block, or
any diff exists in hourly_alert.yml outside the single inserted block.

R4 — Guard tests, mutation-verified red, scoped assertions
New tests/test_ci_artifact_hygiene.py assertions scoped to the named refresh
step block, the exact restore line, and the "Commit hourly artifacts" add
block (never whole-file substring search). Each mutation, one at a time, turns
a new test red: (a) delete the refresh step; (b) move it after render; (c)
remove its fresh gate; (d) remove the pre-invocation rm; (e) remove the
`|| rm -f` failure scrub; (f) add logs/gex_snapshot.json to the restore
arguments; (g) add it to the commit add block; (h) drop `logs/` gitignore
coverage (`git check-ignore logs/gex_snapshot.json` stops succeeding).
FAIL: Any of mutations (a)-(h) leaves the new tests green.
