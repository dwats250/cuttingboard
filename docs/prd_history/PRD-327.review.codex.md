**Reviewed head:** a0a116bb22adb8630b84f5736e624e3dd080292b  **Merge base:** f555b48  
**Verdict:** REJECT — 6 REQUIRED, 2 RECOMMENDED

## Strengths

- The frozen head resolves exactly, HEAD was pinned to it, and `git diff f555b48..a0a116b` contains only the PRD, evidence, and lifecycle bookkeeping. There is no implementation.
- The design correctly keeps `#verdict-zone` as the sole permission surface. Decision word, verdict line, WHY, kill-switch, permission, regime, freshness identifier, staleness element, and coherence warning all remain assigned to VERDICT.
- The proposed one-production-file cone is credible in principle. Existing values needed for Concept A or B are already local to `render_dashboard_html`; no producer, schema, contract, runtime, candidate-card, chart, or deep-SPY change is inherently required.
- The prototype generator reproduced its committed representative outputs byte-for-byte (`cmp -s` returned 0 for the D1 baseline, Concept A primary, and all three committed Concept B renders). Its below-seam assertion also succeeds for the prototypes it generates.
- Macro pressure is not wrongly treated as duplicate. The above-fold pressure line has four named states (`dashboard_renderer.py:1668-1676`), while deep `#macro-tape` uses a different three-component vocabulary and can omit phrases (`dashboard_renderer.py:3406-3428`).
- With a correct viewport, both concepts do materially improve placement. The compression objective is valid; the rejection concerns the chosen hierarchy, evidence, and guards.

## REQUIRED findings

### REQ-1 — [factual drift] The committed measurements are not 390x844 and are captured before the staleness banner settles

**Claim:** The evidence labelled “390x844” does not measure a 390x844 CSS viewport, and its reporter samples the DOM before the historical staleness banner becomes visible.

**Evidence:**

- `measurements.md:1-12` says the measurements use a 390x844 viewport and include the visible staleness banner.
- `measure.py:30-36` runs the geometry reporter synchronously as the injected script is parsed. It does not wait for `DOMContentLoaded` or the staleness script to update the banner.
- `measure.py:38-50` passes `--window-size=390,844`, but never asserts `window.innerWidth` or `window.innerHeight`.
- Every reproduced JSON reported:

  ```text
  "vw": 500
  "vh": 757
  ```

  Thus the `@media(max-width:430px)` rules were not active, and the effective first viewport ended at 757 CSS px.
- Reproduction command:

  ```text
  .venv/bin/python docs/prd_history/PRD-327.evidence/measure.py <matrix HTML files>
  ```

  reproduced the committed tables exactly, but the emitted JSON exposed the 500x757 viewport.
- The same JSON recorded `staleness.hidden: true`. A settled browser inspection later showed `hidden: false`, height 26 px, with a 34 px downstream displacement including spacing.
- Consequently, the statement at `PRD-327.md:433-435` that chart top 840 enters an 844px viewport by 4px is unsupported by that run: its actual viewport was only 757px high.
- Chrome device emulation at an asserted, settled `vw=390`, `vh=844` produced:

  ```text
  D1 stay-flat:  WATCHING 641, header 864, chart 1007
  Concept B:     WATCHING 402, header 625, chart 768
  ```

  The conclusion happens to remain favorable, but the submitted numbers, percentage, viewport interpretation, and “~48 px” staleness adjustment are not the measurements claimed.

**Smallest fix:** Make the measurer use Chrome device metrics, wait until the staleness handler has settled, and fail unless `innerWidth == 390` and `innerHeight == 844`. Regenerate every baseline/concept table and capture. Record settled historical and fresh-banner-hidden states separately instead of estimating one from the other.

### REQ-2 — [scope creep] Correct measurement removes the stated necessity for Concept B, while Concept B weakens TODAY/event orientation

**Claim:** Concept B is larger than necessary for the owner’s stated objective. The argument that only Concept B brings the primary identity into the first viewport is false at a true 390x844 viewport.

**Evidence:**

- Concept A deliberately preserves the TAPE and TODAY hierarchy and has the lowest semantic risk (`PRD-327.md:405-416`).
- The recommendation says Concept B is “the only bounded option” that puts primary identity clearly inside the viewport (`PRD-327.md:440-443`).
- The PRD itself says meaningful chart visibility is not required and remains only a preference (`PRD-327.md:478-482`).
- At a settled, asserted 390x844 viewport with the historical banner visible, Concept A measured:

  ```text
  case                         WATCHING   primary header   chart
  stay-flat                    509        732              875
  locked                       529        752              862
  permitted                    491        714              857
  ```

  All three primary headers enter the first viewport by 92–130 px. Concept A therefore satisfies the primary-identity objective without superseding PRD-318’s TAPE/TODAY hierarchy.
- Concept B removes the visible TAPE and TODAY headings (`PRD-327.md:421-423`). The retained subgroup IDs are invisible DOM metadata, not human-facing orientation.
- Event risk is materially demoted. At true 390px, D1’s ordinary TODAY block was 94px high; Concept B’s TODAY subgroup was 23px. D1 gives TODAY its own bordered/padded operator block and h2 (`dashboard_renderer.py:854-860`, `1011-1015`, `1084-1089`). Concept B reduces EVENT RISK to a `.ctx-cap` at `.64rem` after a subtle rule (`proto_gen.py:56-65`, `111-120`), versus the base `.label` at `.75rem`.
- This conflicts with the design’s own manual criterion that no risk or unavailable state become quieter than D1 (`PRD-327.md:391-401`).

**Smallest fix:** Rebase the recommendation on corrected measurements and select Concept A unless Helm explicitly sets a stronger target such as chart entry or WATCHING ≤460px. If Concept B remains a Gate-A option, retain visible TAPE/TODAY subgroup orientation and restore event-risk prominence, then measure that corrected prototype.

### REQ-3 — [factual drift] D2-Q2 can remove the only rendered per-symbol trend coverage in lineage and inactive states

**Claim:** The assertion that suppressed placeholder symbols always remain in `#trend-structure` is false. Under mixed/unhealthy lineage and inactive-session branches, the deep block does not enumerate symbols.

**Evidence:**

- D2-Q2 suppresses all six chips whenever no row is computed (`PRD-327.md:58-65`).
- R7 says all six symbols remain in deep `#trend-structure` (`PRD-327.md:284-291`).
- At the merge base, unhealthy lineage renders only the lineage-unavailable sentence and bypasses the table (`dashboard_renderer.py:3538-3543`).
- Inactive session similarly bypasses the table (`dashboard_renderer.py:3544-3546`).
- Symbol enumeration does not begin until the separate normal branch at `dashboard_renderer.py:3569-3579`.
- Exact rendered comparison for `coherence_mixed`:

  ```text
  D1 TAPE: SPY=1 QQQ=1 GDX=1 SLV=1 GLD=1 XLE=1
  D1 deep #trend-structure: all six = 0
  Concept B TAPE: all six = 0
  Concept B deep #trend-structure: all six = 0
  ```

- `proto_gen.py:38` classifies this as no computed trend, and `proto_gen.py:109-110` suppresses the chip strip. The result removes those six symbol observations from the entire rendered document, not merely from above WATCHING.
- This is especially unsafe in the lineage-failure state whose prominence the owner explicitly required.

**Smallest fix:** Narrow D2-Q2 so chips are suppressed only when the unchanged deep block actually enumerates every curated symbol. Because changing deep blocks is outside scope, keep the six chips for unhealthy-lineage and inactive-session renders. Add rendered tests for `coherence_mixed`, `session_inactive`, `trend_awaiting_data`, and active no-data states.

### REQ-5 — [derived semantics] The no-synthesis test accepts synthetic judgments made from existing words, and the measured prototype violates the specified caption contract

**Claim:** R8’s token-subset strategy is not fail-closed against a combined market judgment.

**Evidence:**

- R8 defines acceptable context text as a subset of base TAPE/TODAY text plus a caption vocabulary (`PRD-327.md:293-304`).
- The test strategy explicitly calls for a “token-subset test” (`PRD-327.md:382-383`).
- A new element such as “Macro trend bullish” can be assembled entirely from tokens already present in a healthy base render. It can pass a set-style token-subset test and avoids every listed forbidden word.
- R4/R5 assert base values per row but do not explicitly prohibit an additional summary node made from those values.
- The claimed mutation “rename `GEX` caption to `GEX · CONTEXT ONLY`” (`PRD-327.md:487-491`) is also not guaranteed to fail: `CONTEXT ONLY` already occurs in the base TAPE text, so the subset and forbidden-word checks accept it unless caption identity is independently exact-pinned.
- The actual measured Concept B prototype is already non-conforming: `proto_gen.py:111` emits `GEX · CTX ONLY`, while R4 requires caption `GEX` (`PRD-327.md:251-253`) and R8’s caption set contains only `GEX` (`PRD-327.md:296-298`).

**Smallest fix:** Regenerate the prototype with the exact proposed captions. Test the exact ordered child shape, exact caption strings, exact row count, exact values/attributes, and absence of any additional visible text node. Add mutations using only allowed/base tokens and the old `GEX · CONTEXT ONLY` suffix; both must go RED.

### REQ-6 — [factual drift] The binding CSS ceiling overlaps the supposedly frozen phone block

**Claim:** The line-addressed implementation cone misidentifies the phone block and names a renderer symbol that does not exist.

**Evidence:**

- `PRD-327.md:67-76` permits non-phone `_CSS` changes through base lines 854-1092 and says `_PRD318_PHONE_BLOCK` occupies lines 1093-1107.
- At exact base, the `@media(max-width:430px)` block begins at `dashboard_renderer.py:1084` and ends at line 1102.
- The stated permitted range therefore overlaps nine lines of the frozen phone block, `1084-1092`.
- `_PRD318_PHONE_BLOCK` is not a production symbol. It is a literal test constant defined at `tests/test_dashboard_renderer.py:3985`; `rg` finds no definition in `dashboard_renderer.py`.
- This ambiguity matters because the invalid measurement never exercised that phone block.

**Smallest fix:** Replace drifting line ranges with exact structural boundaries or a source hash. At this base, non-phone `_CSS` ends at line 1083 and the phone block is lines 1084-1102. Describe `_PRD318_PHONE_BLOCK` as the test-side byte pin, not a renderer symbol.

## Attack-point dispositions 1–12

1. **Semantic information loss — FAILS.** In mixed/inactive states, the six trend symbols disappear from both TAPE and DETAILS. Visible TAPE/TODAY orientation is also removed from Concept B.

2. **Verdict/permission boundary — HOLDS.** VERDICT remains first and undivided; permission, WHY, kill-switch, freshness, staleness, and lineage remain in `#verdict-zone`. No context conditional is authorized to key on permission or lock state.

3. **Synthetic-context risk — FAILS.** The current prototype contains no explicit synthetic sentence, but the merged visual block has no visible TAPE/TODAY subgroup labels, and the token-subset guard permits a new judgment assembled from existing vocabulary.

4. **Event-risk prominence — FAILS.** TODAY changes from a dedicated 94–130px operator block to a 23–41px subgroup row with smaller caption text and only a subtle separator.

5. **Unavailable-state prominence — FAILS.** Exact strings are generally retained, but `Event schedule unavailable` receives the same material demotion as event risk. Lineage prominence itself remains strong.

6. **Misleading mobile optimization — FAILS.** The committed method measured 500x757 while reporting 390x844, did not activate the phone block, and evaluated chart entry against 844 rather than its actual 757px viewport.

7. **Pixel measurement support — FAILS.** The tables reproduce, but reproduce the wrong viewport and pre-staleness timing. Correct settled 390x844 numbers differ materially.

8. **Deeper-evidence existence — FAILS.** Macro pressure does exist deeper, but curated trend symbols do not exist in deep mixed-lineage or inactive-session branches at `f555b48`.

9. **Implementation cone — FAILS.** Renderer-only implementation is credible, but the CSS ceiling overlaps the frozen phone block and the golden constants are invalid.

10. **Leakage into WATCHING / D1 / SPY / future SPY chart work — HOLDS at the design-authority level.** The PRD expressly excludes every surface from `#watching-zone` onward and any future SPY chart (`PRD-327.md:146-164`). The executable invariance guard must be corrected before implementation.

11. **Test vacuity — FAILS.** R8 token subsets admit recombined judgments and old captions; R9 counts admit arbitrary WATCHING byte changes; partial-computed trend gating lacks a mandatory rendered boundary case.

12. **Right-sizing — FAILS; larger than necessary.** At a true settled 390x844 viewport, lower-risk Concept A already puts all three primary headers inside the viewport. Concept B’s extra hierarchy ruling is not justified by the stated objective or the submitted evidence.
