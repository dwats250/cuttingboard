WHY NOW
PRD-334 repaired the dashboard composition and PRD-335 completed a first slice of
market context. Production inspection then surfaced the last small class of
irritants before live use: the compact macro families do not render four across
on a Pixel-class phone; the peripheral rate/FX/futures/crypto context is
incomplete (no 5Y, no EURUSD/USDCAD, no NG, no ETH); the Sep-6 live run logged a

SPY pre-open copy is verbose on mobile; the Market Context card reads as an
afterthought; History hides its most useful panels behind a disclosure; and the
Trade Vehicles price grid duplicates the richer Trend Structure rows. This PRD
completes the peripheral observations and removes those irritants. It is the LAST
bounded cockpit-polish pass before the dashboard is used in a real market
session. It adds NO decision authority.

packet (audits/prd-336-cockpit-context-polish-material-packet-2026-09/PLAN.md)
