OWNER RULINGS RECORDED (charge 2026-09-02, verbatim intent)
- O1. A hidden secondary tier remains collapsed on initial page load. Once the
  operator explicitly expands that tier, the candidate's observational evidence
  (LEVEL MAP and CHART) is immediately inspectable; both remain individually
  collapsible afterward. This is a NARROW supersession of "secondary charts do
  not auto-open": no automatic secondary expansion on page load; default-open
  chart + level map only after explicit tier expansion. Not generalized beyond
  user-opened hidden candidate tiers.
- O2. SPY Session Observation is CURRENT ORIENTATION, not history. It must not
  require opening DETAILS / HISTORY. It becomes a first-class visible
  observational section before DETAILS / HISTORY. Unrelated deep evidence does
  not move with it.
- O3. SPY Session Observation is OBSERVATIONAL CONTEXT. It does not grant,
  imply, modify, or influence TRADE permission. No second permission authority.
- O4. A SPY chart and a SPY level ladder are provided only from EXISTING data
  and EXISTING renderer semantics; if either needs new acquisition,
  persistence, producer behaviour, cross-run reuse, a new freshness policy,
  fabricated/approximate bars, or candidate/trade semantics, it is DEFERRED
  with its smallest prerequisite named.
- O5. Strong preference for NO new JavaScript. Candidate ranking, grading,
  admission, primary selection, chart contents, ladder contents and permission
  logic do not change. D1 and D2 are not reopened.

WHY NOW
Two usability defects observed on the live phone dashboard after D1/D2: (1) a
C-tier candidate needs three disclosure clicks (tier, LEVEL MAP, CHART) before
its chart is visible; (2) SPY SESSION OBSERVATION is nested inside the
default-collapsed DETAILS / HISTORY block although it is current orientation.
