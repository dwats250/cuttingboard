WHY NOW
A1-P (PRD-323, COMPLETE @ 6847382) shipped the producer half: the hourly run

DIVERGENCE FROM THE A1 PACKET (evidence-derived; packet Section 6 predates A1-P's
final implementation)
1. Selector name/home. The packet proposed a pure `select_chartable_primary(...)`
   living in `setup_chart.py`. A1-P instead shipped
   `select_primary_card_symbol(market_map, price_bars_by_symbol, integrator_skips)`
   in a NEW renderer-free leaf `cuttingboard/delivery/primary_selection.py:55`.
   A1-C's rewire and FILES target that shipped reality, not the packet's proposal.
2. Duplicate authority is an inline latch, not a call. The render site does NOT
   call the leaf; it uses an inline `_chart_slot_open` latch
   (`dashboard_renderer.py:3015` init, `:3141` passed as `chart_slot_available`,
   `:3143` flipped to False by the first card whose SVG is non-empty). That latch
   IS the leaf's algorithm re-implemented inline. A1-C deletes it and routes the
   chart-slot award through the leaf.
3. `_TIER_DEFS`/`_GRADE_ORDER` duplication. A1-P DUPLICATED these into the leaf
   (`primary_selection.py:26-39`) verbatim from the renderer
   (`dashboard_renderer.py:844`), annotated "A1-C removes that duplication when it
   rewires the renderer to this leaf." Because R1 forbids the leaf importing the
   renderer, the single canonical home must be the leaf; A1-C promotes them to
   public names on the leaf and has the renderer import them, deleting the
   renderer's copies (see R7).
4. Derivation home. The packet put the 1m->5m derivation in `setup_chart.py`. That
   module's contract is explicit: "Pure presentation ... computes nothing but
   geometry" (`setup_chart.py:1-17`). A resample is data logic, not geometry, so
   A1-C adds a new small pure leaf for derivation + admission and keeps
   `setup_chart` geometry-only (see ARCHITECTURE RULING).
5. setup_chart reuse is real but bounded. The packet said `render_setup_chart_svg`
   "is NOT interval-agnostic ... trailing 40-bar contract." The only hard blocker
   is `MAX_BARS = 40` (`setup_chart.py:59,98`): a full regular session is ~78
   completed 5m bars, which the daily cap would truncate. The x-axis label is NOT a
   blocker -- `_day_label` (`:73-83`) only reformats `YYYY-MM-DD`-shaped strings and
   passes a short `"HH:MM"` through unchanged. So the setup_chart extension shrinks
   to one default-preserving keyword (`max_bars`), with the derivation leaf emitting
   ET `"HH:MM"` bar labels (see R4, R8).

ARCHITECTURE RULING (per the charge: setup_chart.py vs a small pure helper;
keep dashboard_renderer.py thin; no architecture for neatness)
Three distinct pieces of new logic are placed by module contract, not by neatness:
- 1m->5m derivation + freshness/session admission -> a NEW small pure leaf
  `cuttingboard/delivery/intraday_bars.py` (clock injected, no I/O). This mirrors
  the `primary_selection.py` leaf precedent A1-P just established, is unit-testable
  without the renderer, and keeps `setup_chart`'s geometry-only contract intact.
- Full-session rendering -> EXTEND `setup_chart.render_setup_chart_svg` with one
  default-`MAX_BARS` `max_bars` keyword (this IS geometry, so it belongs here). The
  daily path stays byte-identical.
- Thin sidecar load + the chart-slot branch + the selection rewire -> the renderer,
  kept minimal by delegating derivation/admission to the leaf and rendering to
  setup_chart. The renderer's NET addition is bounded because the rewire DELETES the
  inline latch it replaces.

GOAL
On a fresh, admitted A1-P snapshot for the render pass's canonical primary symbol,
render a deterministic full current-session 5-minute intraday chart -- derived from
the validated 1m source truth -- in that card's single existing chart slot, IN
PLACE OF the daily chart, with an honest ET session / through-time caption and the
compact level ladder retained as subordinate context. When the snapshot is absent,
stale, structurally invalid, session-mismatched, or its `primary_symbol` disagrees
with the render pass's primary, preserve the current daily chart + compact ladder
byte-identically. Display-only: no decision, regime, qualification, ranking,
sizing, contract selection, notification, or acquisition effect.

REQUIREMENTS

R1 -- Sidecar loading contract (thin, never-raises)
`dashboard_renderer._load_intraday_bars_snapshot(path)` mirrors
`_load_price_bars_snapshot` (`:1152-1167`): returns the parsed dict on a
well-formed sidecar and `None` on a missing file, unreadable/corrupt JSON, or a
top-level shape that is not the A1-P envelope (`schema_version`, `generated_at`,
`session_date`, `primary_symbol`, `source`, `columns`, `symbols`). It NEVER raises
into the render path.
FAIL: a missing, truncated, or malformed `logs/intraday_bars_snapshot.json` raises
out of the loader or aborts the dashboard render.

R2 -- Freshness / session admission (pure; clock injected; reconciled with A1-P)
A symbol's bars are ADMITTED for intraday rendering only if ALL hold, evaluated in
the leaf against an injected `now` (ET):
- `session_date` equals the current ET regular-session date;
- `generated_at` (tz-aware UTC per A1-P `runtime/__init__.py:2746`) is not in the
  future and its age relative to `now` is <= 90 minutes (snapshot-write freshness);
- the symbol's per-symbol `through` (tz-aware ISO ts per A1-P
  `runtime/__init__.py:2740`) is within the current ET regular session, not older
  than 90 minutes relative to `now`, and not more than 5 minutes in the future
  (data recency + clock-skew tolerance).
Both bounds bind (generated_at guards write freshness; through guards per-symbol
data recency) -- this is the packet Section 6 / Helm ruling 4 policy verbatim, not a
new invention. Any structural failure (missing/naive/unparseable `generated_at`,
`session_date`, or `through`) omits the whole symbol.
FAIL: a symbol is admitted when its `session_date` differs from the current ET
session, or `generated_at` age > 90m, or `through` is > 90m old, > 5m in the
future, or outside the session; OR a structural failure admits the symbol instead
of omitting it.

R3 -- Deterministic completed-5m derivation (pure)
`intraday_bars` derives 5-minute candles from a symbol's validated 1m bars by
right-labeled, 09:30-ET-anchored regular-session bins: bin k covers 1m bars whose
ts falls in `[09:30 + 5k, 09:30 + 5(k+1))` minutes; O = first bar's Open, H = max
High, L = min Low, C = last bar's Close, V = sum Volume. The derivation is pure and
deterministic: identical 1m input yields byte-identical 5m output; it makes no
provider call and reads only the sidecar bars passed to it.
FAIL: the 5m aggregation uses any rule other than first-Open / max-High / min-Low /
last-Close / sum-Volume over 09:30-anchored 5-minute bins; OR the same 1m input
produces two different 5m outputs across runs.

R4 -- Only COMPLETED bins; never a partial current bin
A 5m bin is rendered only if its right edge is <= the symbol's `through` (i.e., the
bin is fully covered by observed 1m truth). The in-progress bin containing
`through` is excluded. The leaf emits each rendered bin's label as an ET `"HH:MM"`
string in bar-slot 0 (the bin-start), which flows through
`setup_chart._day_label`'s short-string passthrough (`:73-83`) unchanged.
FAIL: a bin whose right edge exceeds `through` (a partial/in-progress bin) is
rendered; OR a bin-start label is emitted as a date string or a value
`_day_label` would reformat.

R5 -- Input-source parity + rewire to the shared leaf (A1-P deferred item)
The renderer computes the chart-slot primary ONCE per pass via
`select_primary_card_symbol(market_map, price_bars_by_symbol, integrator_skips)`,
fed the byte-identical runtime inputs the inline `_chart_slot_open` latch consumed
(the `_price_bars_by_symbol` map at `:1187`, the market map, and integrator skips).
The card whose symbol equals that result takes the single chart slot; the inline
`_chart_slot_open` latch (`:3015,:3141,:3143`) is DELETED. A red test proves the
leaf's winner over the renderer's actual runtime inputs equals the pre-rewire
inline winner across representative states (extending the PRD-323 predicate parity
`tests/test_primary_selection.py:155` to runtime input-source parity).
FAIL: the render site retains any inline chart-slot selection authority after the
rewire; OR the leaf, fed the renderer's runtime inputs, selects a different
chart-slot winner than the pre-rewire inline latch on the parity fixtures; OR the
rendered dashboard's chart-slot symbol changes for any pre-A1-C golden state.

R6 -- Producer / rendered primary agreement (no divergence)
The intraday chart is rendered for a card only if that card is the render pass's
canonical primary (R5) AND the snapshot's recorded `primary_symbol` equals that
symbol AND that symbol is admitted (R2). If the snapshot's `primary_symbol`
disagrees with the render pass's primary, intraday is omitted (honest absence) and
the daily chart is shown.
FAIL: an intraday chart is rendered for a symbol other than the render pass's
canonical primary, or when `snapshot.primary_symbol` differs from that symbol.

R7 -- Single canonical tier defs (duplication removed)
After A1-C, `_TIER_DEFS`/`_GRADE_ORDER` exist in exactly ONE place: public
`TIER_DEFS`/`GRADE_ORDER` on `cuttingboard/delivery/primary_selection.py`. The
renderer imports them; its own copies (`dashboard_renderer.py:844` and the
`_GRADE_ORDER` map) are deleted. The leaf still MUST NOT import
`dashboard_renderer` (PRD-323 R1 preserved).
FAIL: `_TIER_DEFS` or `_GRADE_ORDER` is defined in more than one module after
A1-C; OR `primary_selection` imports `dashboard_renderer`.

R8 -- Full-session render via setup_chart, daily path byte-identical
`render_setup_chart_svg` gains `max_bars: int | None = MAX_BARS`; `None` disables
the `_normalize_bars` trailing cap so a full ~78-bar session renders. Every daily
caller passes nothing, so the default (cap 40, date labels) is byte-identical to
pre-A1-C. The intraday branch passes `max_bars=None` with the leaf's 5m bars +
existing level context (`current_price`, `watch_zones`, `fib_levels`).
FAIL: a daily render differs byte-for-byte from a pre-A1-C golden; OR the intraday
render silently truncates the completed session to 40 bars.

R9 -- Intraday-preferred / daily-fallback in the one existing slot
When R2+R6 admit the primary, its single chart slot shows the 5m intraday SVG
INSTEAD of the daily SVG; the compact level ladder is retained beneath it as
subordinate context. When intraday is not admitted (absent / stale / invalid /
session-mismatched / primary-disagreement), the slot shows the current daily chart
and the same ladder -- no second chart, no second card, no layout change outside
the chart branch.
FAIL: both an intraday and a daily chart render for the same slot; OR a second
chart/card is created; OR the ladder is dropped in either branch; OR any layout
outside the chart branch changes.

R10 -- Honest caption / provenance
The intraday chart carries a truthful caption naming the ET session date and the
per-symbol through-time it actually renders (derived from `session_date` +
`through`); it never claims freshness or coverage beyond the admitted, completed
data. The daily fallback keeps its existing caption.
FAIL: the intraday caption states a session date or through-time not equal to the
admitted snapshot's `session_date` / rendered `through`; OR it is shown on the
daily fallback.

R11 -- Baseline-neutral off the intraday branch
With the sidecar absent, stale, malformed, session-mismatched, or
primary-disagreeing, the rendered dashboard is byte-identical to a pre-A1-C golden
(GEX suppression model: guarded emit, no partial output). Behavior outside the
chart branch is unchanged in every case.
FAIL: any non-admitted sidecar state changes the dashboard output from a pre-A1-C
golden anywhere outside the admitted intraday chart substitution.

R12 -- Decision / notification isolation (G2)
No decision, regime, qualification, setup grade, ranking, position size, contract
selection, notification eligibility, acquisition, or any existing pipeline-owned
artifact changes as a function of A1-C. The consumer is pure display: it reads the
sidecar and renders; it writes nothing and triggers no fetch.
FAIL: any decision-pipeline output, notification, or existing artifact differs when
A1-C is present vs absent for identical inputs.
