**Prioritized findings**

1. **[BLOCKER] Verdict humanization asserts facts the current decision does not establish.**  
   *Taxonomy: derived semantics · Surface: DEGRADED.* “Nothing qualifies” contradicts existing branches for qualified setups that are gated or refused because a contract exceeds the risk budget. “Longs allowed” erases the existing short and momentum-long distinctions. HALT also includes market-stress invalidation, not just missing/failed inputs. “Nothing is permitted” broadens the operator lock beyond its canonical **no new trades** restriction.  
   **Correction:** Translate the already-resolved state and permission; use **“No new trades permitted”** for the generic no-trade/lock statement, retain the existing WHY distinctions, and use **“System halted”** without inventing a universal cause. Preserve visible halt, coherence and kill-switch information even when an operator lock overlaps. Raw `data-*` attributes cannot repair misleading visible text. Evidence: [permission translation](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:1845), [decision and lock presentation](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:2941), [WHY branches](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:2988), [canonical lock](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/config.py:68), [market-stress HALT principle](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/VISION.md:28).

2. **[MAJOR] Removing refresh leaves a newer published decision undiscovered while the old page can look current.**  
   *Taxonomy: stale-data ambiguity · Surface: DEGRADED.* The existing banner threshold is 90 minutes. `setInterval(run,60000)` ages the loaded pipeline timestamp; it neither discovers a new publication nor reruns server-side SPY/GEX admission. The baked inactive-session flag also remains frozen.  
   **Correction:** Retain the interval, but make **“Snapshot — manual reload”** and a usable reload control visible immediately, alongside the absolute timestamp. Say **“Reload to check for an update”**, since a newer board may not exist. Preserve current thresholds and inactive/unavailable branches. Do not make the timer grant/revoke permission or revalidate production GEX. Evidence: [refresh and staleness implementation](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:406), [timestamp source](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3049).

3. **[MAJOR] Market Movement disappears from the proposed inventory.**  
   *Taxonomy: factual drift · Surface: INERT if omitted.* Neither the seven-region proposal nor HISTORY names the existing grouped movement card. Its captured daily changes are not replaced by SMA alignment, RVOL, or “participation captured” counts.  
   **Correction:** Give the existing fragment an explicit destination in MARKET STRUCTURE, retaining its capture time and existing suppression. Do not substitute a newly derived breadth score. Evidence: [current emission](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3586), [movement consumer contract](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/movement_card.py:1).

4. **[MAJOR] Lower Macro Tape is not wholly redundant with Trend Structure.**  
   *Taxonomy: hidden coupling · Surface: INERT for removed quote coverage.* Its six tradable prices come from `market_map`; the rich table reads `trend_structure_snapshot` records and suppresses rows under inactive or unhealthy-lineage branches. Moving the table therefore does not preserve the same quote coverage or source.  
   **Correction:** Delete duplicated driver rows, but retain the existing six-symbol quote projection once in MARKET STRUCTURE, explicitly distinguished from trend snapshot values. Preserve macro-bias suppression, vote tally and pressure availability behavior. **Do not backfill the Trend table from another source to make it appear equivalent.** Evidence: [quote source](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:1703), [tradables emission](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3749), [Trend table branches and source](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3810).

5. **[MAJOR] Market Control distillation hides current invalidation and deletes candidate evidence without proving equivalence.**  
   *Taxonomy: factual drift · Surface: DEGRADED; deleted cells INERT.* Transition and invalidation describe current conditions, so HISTORY is a misleading home. Candidate implication includes ACTIVE/NEAR_MISS/BLOCKED counts. EVENT is separately resolved by the producer; a similar-looking NEXT EVENT strip is not proof of identical evidence.  
   **Correction:** Put the short existing transition/invalidation projection with SPY SESSION, preserving typed-unavailable values. Retain the complete existing six displayed fields inside HISTORY; no additional disclosure is needed. Remove EVENT only after proving its coverage and unavailable/expiring states survive elsewhere. Never derive a new combined permission from these cells. Evidence: [six-field rendering](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3604), [event source](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/market_control_card.py:175), [transition/invalidation derivation](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/market_control_card.py:192).

6. **[MAJOR] “Summary out, profile collapsed” leaves production GEX density unresolved and can make synthetic numbers the apparent current read.**  
   *Taxonomy: schema ambiguity · Surface: DEGRADED.* Production rendering already includes `_profile_block`; collapsing only the reference leaves the large production block expanded. When production suppresses, the frozen reference remains. A SYNTHETIC badge alone is weak protection against reading those strikes as current context.  
   **Correction:** Specify compact versus expanded treatment for **both** fragments. Keep “Frozen synthetic SPX example — not current market data,” the model-sign convention and the SPX/SPY distinction beside visible reference numbers. Retain a separate current-availability indication when production is absent. Scope any “Full GEX details” label to the fragment it controls. Evidence: [production composition](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/gex_card.py:680), [reference composition and guide](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/gex_reference.py:177).

7. **[MAJOR] Blind golden regeneration would erase the evidence intended to protect the non-goals.**  
   *Taxonomy: hidden coupling · Surface: DEGRADED if semantic regressions are accepted.* Whole-file pins, protected-region hashes, GEX absence equality and the embedded A1 chart oracle protect different things. Recomposition does not authorize resetting all of them.  
   **Correction:** PRD-334 must explicitly name authorized presentation/test changes. Preserve the embedded candidate-chart oracle and executable decision, lock, selection and suppression invariants. Establish the recomposed baseline independently of production GEX, then verify absent/stale/invalid GEX equals that baseline. Review fixture diffs before updating hashes; assert visible copy as well as raw attributes. Evidence: [whole-file pins](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/tests/test_dashboard_d2_seam.py:40), [region and embedded-SVG assertions](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/tests/test_dashboard_renderer.py:4744).

8. **[MINOR] “Exactly four disclosures” overlooks existing low-tier and GEX-table controls.**  
   *Taxonomy: factual drift · Surface: DEGRADED.* Low-tier cards still use `<details>`; `_profile_block` contains another disclosure for all bins. Flattening a selected card must not make that selection the canonical primary or give every card the A1 intraday source.  
   **Correction:** Drop the arbitrary disclosure count and enumerate the controls actually retained. Remove the requested card wrappers independently of primary selection, chart neutrality and daily/intraday source assignment. Evidence: [low-tier disclosure](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:3536), [chart source and neutrality](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/dashboard_renderer.py:2480), [GEX table disclosure](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/gex_card.py:436).

9. **[MINOR] Broad-market labeling and colour names overstate what these observations mean.**  
   *Taxonomy: derived semantics · Surface: DEGRADED.* The curated six-symbol set is not broad equity coverage; a rising VIX or yield is not “go,” and a falling instrument is not necessarily “stop.”  
   **Correction:** Label the table **“Trend Structure — curated watch set”**; retain direction labels/arrows separately from permission and pressure text. Keep existing symbol sets and shared macro layout unchanged; perform family regrouping locally in the renderer. Identify gold/silver futures as such. Evidence: [six-symbol set](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/config.py:278), [shared dashboard/notification layout](/home/dustin/Projects/cuttingboard/cuttingboard/delivery/macro_tape_layout.py:1).

The ingestion gaps and Trend intraday VWAP are correctly deferred. New-board notification can also remain deferred, provided the page plainly communicates manual snapshot delivery. The SPY prose change is presentation-only **if it preserves the existing observation-state branches, ORB states and three distinct clocks**.
