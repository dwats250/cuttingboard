ROOT CAUSE
`hidden dependency` -- PRD-323 R1/R2 made the A1-P primary a function of the
hourly `build_market_map` result and `trend_history`, and deferred runtime
input-source parity to A1-C, without documenting that the hourly market map's
`structure_results` and `bar_windows` inputs were themselves gated on
`regime.posture != "STAY_FLAT"` (a pre-existing constraint from the hourly
notify path, not from PRD-323). The producer therefore inherited a
posture-dependent observational input that the renderer's live path never had.
The A1-C consumer (PRD-324) is correct and falls back correctly; the defect is
upstream of it.

GOAL
Under STAY_FLAT the hourly path computes the same observational market
structure the live path computes (structure always; candidates and candidate
OHLCV whenever the regime has a trade direction, exactly as the live path) so
the hourly market map, the A1-P primary, and the A1-P intraday acquisition
converge with the renderer, while every permission and decision surface under
STAY_FLAT stays byte-identical to today.
